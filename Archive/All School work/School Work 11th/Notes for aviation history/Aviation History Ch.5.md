# **Aviation & Cold War Notes (1945–1975)**

**From end of WWII → early Cold War → Vietnam era**

---

# **1. Breaking the Sound Barrier & Early Jet Age**

### **Chuck Yeager**

- First to break the sound barrier (Mach 1) on **Oct 14, 1947**.
- Flew the **Bell X-1 "Glamorous Glennis"** (sometimes written XS-1).
- Aircraft had **thin wings**, **adjustable stabilizer**, and a **rocket engine**.
- Yeager had **broken ribs** before the flight; he visited a **veterinarian** for treatment and used a **broomstick** to close the cockpit door.
- WWII fighter ace; close friend and test partner of **Bob Hoover** (famous test pilot who performed aerobatics in a disabled aircraft).

### **Early Jet Aircraft**

- **Gloster Meteor** – 606 mph; Britain’s first operational jet.
- **Lockheed P-80 Shooting Star** – first U.S. operational jet fighter.
- **GE P-59 Airacomet** – America’s first jet, but underpowered.
- These were considered “speed breakers” paving the way for supersonic flight.

### **Key Technologies**

- **Sweptback wings** developed to delay shockwaves and allow higher speeds.
- **Electronic jamming** of radar and missiles begins.
- **Surface-to-Air Missiles (SAMs)** introduced.
- **Precision-Guided Munitions** begin development.
- **Nuclear destructive power** increases massively (A-bomb → H-bomb).

### **Jet Age Milestone**

- **Sept 18, 1947 – U.S. Air Force becomes a separate military branch.**

# **2. Berlin Airlift (1948–1949)**

### **Context**

- Germany divided after WWII.
- Berlin also divided into East (Soviet) and West (Allied).
- **Summer 1948** – Soviets blockade West Berlin.

### **Airlift**

- U.S. and Allies fly supplies into West Berlin.
- Lasted **247 days**.
- Aircraft used: **C-47s**, **C-54s**, others.
- Blockade lifted in **May 1949**.

---

# **3. Korean War (1950–1953)**

“The Forgotten War”

### **Key Facts**

- 1950: **North Korea invades South Korea.**
- **UN coalition**, led by U.S., responds.
- **Massive ROE restrictions** (could **not** strike inside China).
- Conflict along the **38th Parallel**.

### **Jet Combat**

- First jet-on-jet dogfights.
- **F-86 Sabre vs MiG-15** in “MiG Alley.”
- F-86 pilots had a **10:1 kill ratio** despite being outnumbered.
- F-86 top speed ~**0.95 Mach**.
- Many pilots were veterans, average age ~30.

### **Aircraft Still Used**

- Some WWII aircraft like the **P-51 Mustang** were still flown early in the war.

### **Ceasefire**

- Signed in **1953**; war technically never formally ended.

---

# **4. Cold War Strategic Power (1950s–1960s)**

### **Nuclear Era**

- **Hydrogen bomb (H-bomb)** tested in 1952.
- ~**500× more powerful** than Hiroshima’s A-bomb.

### **Strategic Air Command (SAC)**

- Taken over by **Gen. Curtis LeMay**.
- Built a 24/7 nuclear-armed readiness posture.
- Heavy use of bombers like the:
    - **B-47**
    - **B-52 Stratofortress** (still flying today)

### **Interceptor & Missile Era**

- Fighters: **F-106** and others.
- Heavy use of **Air-to-Air missiles**, moving away from guns.

### **Spy Planes & Skunk Works**

**Kelly Johnson** (legendary engineer) designed:

- **U-2 Dragon Lady** – flew above **70,000 ft**.
- **SR-71 Blackbird** – fastest air-breathing aircraft ever.
- Skunk Works used a **dummy titanium company** to buy Soviet titanium.
- SR-71 ran on **JP-7 fuel**, extremely high flashpoint.

---

# **5. The Cuban Missile Crisis (1962)**

### **Key Points**

- 13-day confrontation in **October 1962**.
- Soviet Union attempted to place **nuclear missiles in Cuba**.
- A **U-2 spy plane** discovered the missile sites.
- Brought the world extremely close to nuclear war.
- Example of a Cold War **proxy crisis**.

### **Policy**

- **MAD – Mutually Assured Destruction** strategy defined the era.
- Nuclear **triad** established:
    - Land-based missiles
    - Submarine-launched missiles
    - Strategic bombers

---

# **6. Vietnam War (1955–1975)**

### **Gulf of Tonkin (1964)**

- U.S. dramatically increases involvement after attack on U.S. ships.

### **Operation Rolling Thunder (1965–1968)**

- Sustained bombing campaign against North Vietnam.
- Considered a **deeply flawed strategy**:
    - Strict political restrictions
    - Targets often ineffective
    - Gave the enemy time to adapt

### **Linebacker I & II (1972)**

- More aggressive and effective bombing operations.
- Aircraft used heavily:
    - **B-52 Stratofortress**
    - **F-105 Thunderchief**
    - **F-4 Phantom II**

### **Vietnam Combat Realities**

- North Vietnam used **sophisticated air defenses**:
    - SAMs
    - Radar
    - AAA
    - Fighters (MiG-17, MiG-21, MiG-19)
- U.S. increased use of **attack helicopters**.
- Significant ROE restrictions similar to Korea.

### **Pilots to Know**

- **Capt. James Jabara** – first American jet ace.
- **Col. Francis "Gabby" Gabreski** – top ace in WWII & Korea.

### **Important Aircraft Mentioned**

- **F-100 Super Sabre**
- **MiG-15**
- **Bell X-15** (high-speed research rocket plane)
- **F-104 Starfighter ("missile with a man in it")** – extremely high speed, small wing.

# **7. Desert Storm Connection**

### **General Chuck Horner**

- Commander of U.S. & Coalition Air Forces in **Operation Desert Storm (1991)**.
- Concerned early on that the **F-117 stealth fighter** might get shot down.
