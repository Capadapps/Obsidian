<u>**Essay preparation and writing:**</u>

In the time period from 1870-1900, many of the greater European powers sought to engage in vast colonial control across Africa and Asia, this had become commonly known as “New imperialism”. Great Britain, France, Germany, Belgium, and other European nations had tried to claim large territories through various means, yet these means had been fundamentally driven by economic motivations throughout Europe.

During the period from 1870-1900, the greater European powers had encountered a large number of economic driving forces that would push the European powers into colonizing and gaining control across Africa and Asia. These major economic incentives were all mainly characterized by the major ideal of the need to colonize due to Superior and Inferior races, as well as the insurance of ethnic prosperity across vast lands in many years to come.
