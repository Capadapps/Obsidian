- Improved sanitation in cities promoted a better quality of life  as well as some improvements in health care, the effective controllability of the diseases was still under development and would require a technological breakthrough in order to contain it.
- Many believed that disease was contracted through the inhalation of smells of things that were decaying.
- Breakthrough in how drinking water and other factors attributed greatly to disease contraction saw many rethink biological disease contraction and how to stop it.
- Louis Pasteur developed the germ theory in the 1850s 
- Usage of a microscope was used to study bacteria and fermentation by Louis and it was found that heat could cause bacteria to die and not effect the drink it was infecting.
- By 1870 Louis and others work majorly influenced others ideas of how disease was contracted and proved a connection between germs and disease.
- German doctor Robert Koch and his coworkers had by 1870 already developed pure cultures of living bacteria and they could be studied to determine their life cycles, so we could better understand how to eradicate them.
- These Breakthroughs led to a number of vaccines being produced, then antibiotics were developed due to further breakthroughs in the middle of the next century.
- After this many improvements could be made to the healthcare infrastructure of the time.
