### DBQ Guidelines:

<u>**Requirements and rubric:**</u>

**Contextualization:**

Describes a broader historical context relevant to the prompt

**Thesis/Claim:**

Responds to the claim with a historically defensible thesis relevant to the prompt.

**Documents:**

**At least three documents** are needed to address the topic of the prompt.

*Quotes are insufficient to earn this point*

**Support:**

Supports an argument in response to the prompt using <u>**at least four documents.**</u>

*These documents should meet and exceed the standard set for the description point*

**Explanation:**

For at lease two documents, explains how or why the documents historical situation, intended audience, point of view, and/or purpose (HPP) is relevant to an argument.

**Evidence beyond the documents:**

The response must describe evidence and must use more than a phrase or reference.

**Complex understanding:**

Use sophisticated argumentation and Effective use of evidence

*More on rubric*
