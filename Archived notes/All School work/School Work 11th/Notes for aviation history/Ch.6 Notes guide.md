# **1. Korean War (1950–1953)**

### *“The Forgotten War”*

### **Overview**

- Began in **1950** when **North Korea invaded South Korea**.
- A **UN coalition** led by the United States intervened.
- Operations were restricted by **Rules of Engagement (ROE)**:
    - **No strikes inside China**, even though Chinese forces were involved.
- Fighting centered around the **38th Parallel**.

### **Jet Age Combat**

- Marked the **first jet-on-jet air combat** in history.
- Most iconic dogfight arena: **“MiG Alley.”**
- **Main aircraft:**
    - **F-86 Sabre (US)**
    - **MiG-15 (USSR/China)**
- **Kill Ratio:**
    - U.S. F-86 pilots achieved a **10:1 kill ratio**, even when outnumbered.
- **Performance:**
    - F-86 top speed ~**0.95 Mach**
    - Pilots were often **WWII-trained**, average age ~30.

### **Legacy Aircraft**

- Early in the war, older WWII fighters like the **P-51 Mustang** were still used.

### **Outcome**

- **1953 armistice** ended active combat.
- No formal peace treaty — the war is technically still ongoing.

# **2. Cold War Strategic Power (1950s–1960s)**

## **The Nuclear Era**

- **Hydrogen bomb (H-bomb)** first tested in **1952**.
- Roughly **500× more powerful** than Hiroshima’s atomic bomb.

## **Strategic Air Command (SAC)**

- Commanded by **General Curtis LeMay**.
- Established **24/7 nuclear alert** with bombers ready to launch at any moment.
- Key bombers:
    - **B-47 Stratojet**
    - **B-52 Stratofortress** — still in service today.

## **Interceptor & Missile Advancements**

- Move from gun-based dogfighting to **air-to-air missiles**.
- Key interceptors included the **F-106 Delta Dart**, among others.

# **3. Skunk Works & Strategic Reconnaissance**

### **Kelly Johnson & Lockheed Skunk Works**

- Brilliant designer responsible for:
    - **U-2 Dragon Lady**
        - Flew over **70,000 ft** to avoid interception.
    - **SR-71 Blackbird**
        - Fastest air-breathing aircraft ever built.
        - Cruised above **Mach 3**.
        - Required special **JP-7 fuel** with high flashpoint.
- Used a **dummy corporation** to secretly buy **Soviet titanium** for SR-71 construction.

# **4. Cuban Missile Crisis (1962)**

### **Key Aspects**

- 13-day standoff in **October 1962**.
- The Soviet Union attempted to place **nuclear missiles in Cuba**.
- A **U-2 reconnaissance flight** revealed missile sites.
- World came closer to nuclear war than ever before.

### **Cold War Strategy**

- **MAD – Mutually Assured Destruction** dominated military thinking.
- Establishment of the **Nuclear Triad**:
    1. Land-based ICBMs
    2. Submarine-launched missiles
    3. Strategic bombers

# **5. Vietnam War (1955–1975)**

## **Escalation**

- **1964 Gulf of Tonkin Incident** led to major U.S. involvement.

## **Operation Rolling Thunder (1965–1968)**

- Sustained bombing of North Vietnam.
- Considered **strategically flawed** due to:
    - Heavy political restrictions
    - Ineffective target selection
    - Allowed enemy adaptation

## **Linebacker I & II (1972)**

- More aggressive and effective bombing operations.
- Key aircraft:
    - **B-52 Stratofortress**
    - **F-105 Thunderchief**
    - **F-4 Phantom II**

## **Vietnam Air Combat Environment**

- North Vietnam employed advanced defenses:
    - **SAMs**
    - **AAA**
    - **Radar systems**
    - **MiG-17, MiG-19, MiG-21 fighters**
- U.S. significantly expanded use of **attack helicopters**, including:
    - **AH-1 Cobra** (Vietnam’s primary attack helicopter)

## **Notable Pilots**

- **Capt. James Jabara** – first American jet ace (Korea).
- **Randy “Duke” Cunningham** – first American ace in Vietnam.
- **Francis “Gabby” Gabreski** – leading ace in WWII & Korea.

## **Important Aircraft**

- **F-100 Super Sabre**
- **MiG-15**
- **Bell X-15** – research aircraft, reached **Mach 6.7**, altitudes ~50 miles
- **F-104 Starfighter** – “missile with a man in it”

# **6. Space Race & Rocketry**

## **Foundational Rocket Scientists**

### **Konstantin Tsiolkovsky (Russia)**

- Early theoretical pioneer of spaceflight.
- Developed multistage rocket concepts and fuel equations.

### **Robert Goddard (USA)**

- Calculated required velocities for orbit.
- Launched first liquid-fueled rocket.
- Work laid groundwork for modern rocketry.

### **Hermann Oberth (Germany)**

- Studied **practical space travel** and inspired early rocket engineers.

### **Wernher von Braun**

- German rocket engineer behind **V-2 rockets** (WWII).
- Came to U.S. via **Operation Paperclip**.
- Developed:
    - **Redstone Rocket** (Huntsville, Alabama)
    - **Saturn V** (Apollo moon missions)
- Considered the **father of modern rocket science**.

### **Sergei Korolev (USSR)**

- Chief Soviet rocket designer.
- Created **R-7 Semyorka**, world’s first **ICBM**.
- Engineered **Sputnik 1**, the first satellite.

# **8. Additional Key Concepts**

### **Berlin Airlift (1948–1949)**

- U.S. & Allies supplied West Berlin entirely by air after a Soviet blockade.

### **Cold War Themes**

- U.S. vs USSR ideological & nuclear rivalry.
- **Proxy wars:** Korea, Vietnam, Cuban Missile Crisis, etc.
- **MAD** shaping strategy.

### **Firsts to Know**

- First operational American jet fighter: **P-80 Shooting Star**
- Primary U.S. fighter in Korea: **F-86 Sabre**
- First war with helicopters widely used: **Korean War**
- First American aces:
    - **Korea:** Capt. James Jabara
    - **Vietnam:** “Duke” Cunningham

### **SR-71 Facts**

- Leaks fuel on the ground (designed to expand at speed).
- Built with Soviet titanium purchased via **fake companies**.
- Cruised above **Mach 3**.
