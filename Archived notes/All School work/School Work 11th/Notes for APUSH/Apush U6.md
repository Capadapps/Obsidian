Custer’s Last Stand:

1. The cause of defeat for custer was of overconfidence in his army, which led to him splitting up the 7th cavalry division and allowed the natives to attack and defeat each group in pieces.

3. The battle resulted in the worst defeat for US forces during the Great Sioux war. This later became a symbol of native resistance.

Big idea: The big idea is that this battle was a united effort between a large group of the tribes opposed to the American expansion and served as a symbol of resistance.
