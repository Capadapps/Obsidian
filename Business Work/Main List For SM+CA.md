<u>**Swipes are great for increasing engagement**</u><u> -</u> likes and replies plus you can prompt people to share it with friends or you can put a CTA (Call to action) to take a certain action such as book a call, link in bio… etc.

<u>**Reels -**</u> Reels are best for growing an account. You can easily make them with tools such as Capcut. To get reel content either take any long form content they have already - most influencers have podcasts and other videos. If you’re working with a local business or friends and family its best if you go to them and either 1) give them content to film or 2) give them the content idea plus film it then edit. <u>***7 to 50 second long reels do the best.***</u>

<u>**Types of reels:**</u>

- Before and after
- Day in the life
- Behind the scenes
- Tutorial/how to
- Life Story
- Event promotion/product promotion
- Text over lifestyle video
- Common questions/FAQ

Make sure to have a strong hook to capture the viewers attention. Achieve this with text or with your clients voice.

NOBODY cares about a video that starts like, “Hey my name is Jeff. I’m a dentist who specializes in blah blah.”

EVERYONE cares about a video that starts like, “3 ways to NEVER get cavities ever again etc…” then end the reel with some kind of CTA or action.

**List of actions to take with reels:**

- Follow to learn more
- Encourage them to leave a comment about whatever was talked about in the reel.
- Book a call today by clicking the link in my bio
- Come in today for XYZ

<u>Stories - </u>Stories are a great way to engage with your audience, sell products, book calls, and let people in behind the scenes.

**Types of Stories:**

**8 Steps to Building our first offer**

### **Step 1: Understand your client’s needs.**

1. **Initial Consultation**
- <u>Schedule a meeting to discuss the clients business goals and current website presence.</u>
- <u>Ask The client questions to understand their pain points and objectives. Such as their biggest goal.</u>

**Example Questions:**

- Are there any specific problems you’re encountering with your business and website growth?
- What are your main products or services? (Helps you know What products to promo on their site)
- What is your budget for a website and sales service? **<**** *****KNOW BEFORE GOING INTO CLOSING MEETING TO ADJUST THE PRICE BEFOREHAND.***

2. **Research Competitors**
