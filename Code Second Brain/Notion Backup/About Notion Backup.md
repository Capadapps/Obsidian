Tell Claude: *"refresh my Notion backups"* or *"back up <page> to Obsidian"*. Claude fetches the page via the Notion connector and mirrors it here with a `notion-url` and `backed-up` date in the frontmatter.

Don't edit mirror files by hand — edit in Notion, then refresh.
