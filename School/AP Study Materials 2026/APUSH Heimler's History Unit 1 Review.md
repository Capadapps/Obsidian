1. Society/ Politics of Indigenous people
The native populations in the Americas were diverse and varied, and geography played a very important role in the development of those differences.
* Maize - first cultivated in 5000bce, became staple crop of people in the Americas and was a large factor in the growth of indigenous thriving. - Econ development, Permanent settlements (not nomadic), Advanced irrigation, social diversification.
Examples: Iroquois, Cherokee, Messapian (larger groups 4k-6k) Hot/ dry climate Indians developed nomadic lifestyles to move according to seasons, teepee for these hot climates.
