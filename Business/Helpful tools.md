### Free CRM Tools

1. **HubSpot CRM**
    - **Features**: Contact management, deal tracking, task management, email tracking, meeting scheduling, and more.
    - **Pros**: Comprehensive features, user-friendly interface, integrates well with other tools.
    - **Cons**: Some advanced features are behind a paywall, but the free version is robust enough for basic needs.
2. **Zoho CRM**
    - **Features**: Lead management, sales automation, workflow automation, reporting and analytics.
    - **Pros**: Customizable and offers a wide range of features in the free tier.
    - **Cons**: Slight learning curve, some features limited in the free version.
3. **Bitrix24**
    - **Features**: CRM, project management, communication tools, contact management.
    - **Pros**: Extensive features including collaboration tools, good for team management.
    - **Cons**: Interface can be overwhelming due to the vast number of features.

### Free Email Campaign Tools

4. **Mailchimp (Free Plan)**
    - **Features**: Email campaigns, basic templates, audience segmentation, performance reports.
    - **Pros**: Easy to use, integrates with various other tools, great for beginners.
    - **Cons**: Limited to 500 contacts and 1,000 emails per month on the free plan.
5. **Sendinblue (Free Plan)**
    - **Features**: Email campaigns, transactional emails, SMS marketing, basic templates, marketing automation.
    - **Pros**: Unlimited contacts, allows up to 300 emails per day.
    - **Cons**: Daily email sending limit on the free plan.
6. **MailerLite (Free Plan)**
    - **Features**: Email campaigns, landing pages, pop-ups, email automation, reporting.
    - **Pros**: Easy to use, offers up to 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 emails per month.
    - **Cons**: Some advanced features and templates are limited to the paid plans.

### How to Use These Tools Effectively

7. **CRM Tools**:
    - **HubSpot CRM**: Use it to manage your contact list, track interactions, and set reminders for follow-ups. Utilize the email tracking and scheduling features to stay on top of your outreach efforts.
    - **Zoho CRM**: Customize the CRM to fit your specific needs. Use the lead management and workflow automation to streamline your process.
    - **Bitrix24**: Leverage the project management and communication tools for team collaboration if you have partners or assistants.
8. **Email Campaign Tools**:
    - **Mailchimp**: Set up automated email sequences for follow-ups and use the audience segmentation to send personalized emails.
    - **Sendinblue**: Utilize the marketing automation features to create workflows that nurture your leads.
    - **MailerLite**: Take advantage of the landing page and pop-up builders to capture leads and grow your email list.
